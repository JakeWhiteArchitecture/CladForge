2 · Elevations

Click a wall face to add its coplanar patch to the active elevation; click again to remove it. Click a face round a corner and it becomes the next elevation in the chain, with the coursing carried round. Faces that don't meet the chain start a new one. Openings punched by the importer become holes.

Stuck or misbehaving? Run cladforge() in the browser console for the engine state and a log of every extraction.

Picking generates nothing on its own. When the chain is complete, press Enter or use Make chain at the top right of the view to choose the cladding and build it. After that the whole panel edits live.

3 · Buildup

Mitred cuts the whole buildup on the corner's bisector. Master lap is a panel detail: one board runs past and the other butts behind it, leaving the joint gap exposed. Pick which board masters on the corner row above.

Cover / bay: 158 mm

Openings

Panel edges land on the window jambs and each span between them is split into equal bays no wider than the maximum panel, so a window that does not suit the panel centres still sets the joints. A solid timber cavity closer goes to both vertical sides of every opening. Heads and sills are not lined.

4 · Panels

Panel width drives batten centres: joints land on a batten, intermediate battens keep spans under 600 mm. Horizontal joints are open joints at the gap. Noggins behind them are only generated where counter-battens are on: between vertical battens a noggin sits on the drainage plane and dams it.

4 · Planks

Lap > 0 courses at face width minus lap (lapped profiles); lap = 0 courses at face width plus gap (open-jointed). Courses start at the splash zone and work up. End joints land on battens and stagger course to course.
